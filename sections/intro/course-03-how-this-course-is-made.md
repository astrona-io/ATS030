# How This Course Is Made

Authors map every section to the official Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD) objectives and review mechanisms against primary documentation. They check examples for component responsibility and questions for one supported answer.

Structural checks verify reading paths, section introductions, module summaries, draft front matter and YAML contracts. Technical pages remain draft until review. No executed commands, sample runtime output or laboratory validation are claimed.

The [contributors](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS030/graphs/contributors) maintain the material. To [report a mistake](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS030/issues), identify the page and statement, and explain the expected behavior. The repository [license](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS030/blob/main/LICENSE) governs reuse.

Official objectives belong to their respective owners. Teaching and knowledge checks are independently authored learning material.
